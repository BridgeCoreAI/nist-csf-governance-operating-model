## Worked Example

### Evidence
Approved synthetic meeting minutes show that stakeholder expectations were discussed and recorded.

### Condition
Stakeholder needs are documented through a formal governance meeting.

### Specific Gap
No evidence demonstrates that stakeholder expectations are reviewed on a recurring basis or tracked through completion.

### Finding
Stakeholder expectations are documented and considered, but recurring review and action closure are not demonstrated.

### Recommendation
Establish a recurring review process for stakeholder expectations and related governance actions.

### How to Operationalize
Assign a governance owner, maintain a stakeholder register, conduct quarterly reviews, record changes, assign action owners and due dates, and retain evidence of closure.

### Completion Measure
All active stakeholder expectations have an owner, current review date, status, and linked completion evidence.
