# Glossary

**GOVERN**: the NIST CSF 2.0 function establishing and monitoring an organization's cybersecurity risk management strategy, expectations, and policy.

**GV.OC**: Organizational Context, one category within GOVERN, covering mission alignment, stakeholder understanding, legal/regulatory/contractual obligations, and critical service/dependency awareness.

**Control Status**: Pass, Partial Pass, Fail, or Not Assessed; whether a control is currently meeting its requirement.

**Maturity**: a separate five-level assessment of how consistently and reliably a control operates over time, independent of current status.

**Design Assessment**: whether a control, as specified, is capable of achieving its intent.

**Operating-Effectiveness Assessment**: whether a control is actually being followed, consistently, with evidence.

**Evidence Record**: a structured artifact stating what specific evidence demonstrates, its source, owner, and limitations.

**Finding**: a documented gap, tied to specific evidence, distinguishing what is required from what is currently true.

**Recommendation**: a proposed fix addressing the exact gap identified in a finding, including an operationalization plan.

**Operationalization**: the practical plan for acting on a recommendation: owner, process, cadence, tracking method, required artifact, completion measure, completion evidence, revalidation requirement.

**Synthetic Example**: fictional data used for illustration, explicitly labeled, not representing any real organization or event.
