# Evidence Model

## Purpose

An evidence record exists to answer one question precisely: what does this specific artifact demonstrate, and what does it not demonstrate? Evidence that cannot answer that question clearly is not useful evidence, regardless of how much documentation surrounds it.

## Evidence Record Structure

See `templates/evidence-record-template.md` for the full structure. Every evidence record states: the control it supports, its type, owner, source, dates, review period, retention period, classification, and whether it is a synthetic example. It also states what the evidence demonstrates, its limitations, and revalidation requirements.

## Classification

Every public evidence record in this repository carries:

Classification: Public
Synthetic Example: Yes

This metadata is descriptive. It does not itself enforce access control or prove evidence validity; it tells a reader what they are looking at.

## What Evidence Cannot Do

The existence of an evidence record does not, by itself, prove a control is operating effectively. Operating effectiveness requires evidence sustained over time, reviewed on a cadence, and revalidated as circumstances change. A single approved document from one point in time is a snapshot, not an ongoing guarantee.
