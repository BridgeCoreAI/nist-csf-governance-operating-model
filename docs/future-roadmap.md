## Versioning Convention

- **Major**: significant operating-model changes
- **Minor**: new GOVERN categories, controls, templates, registers, or workflows
- **Patch**: corrections, clarifications, formatting improvements, or minor evidence-model changes

No future GOVERN category is fully developed until it is the subject of an explicitly scoped, approved release batch. This roadmap describes intent, not committed content.
